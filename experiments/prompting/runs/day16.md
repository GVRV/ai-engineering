### How to explain it in class

1. **Introduce chloroplasts**
   - Show a diagram of a leaf cell with chloroplasts.
   - Explain that chloroplasts are the “kitchen” where food (glucose) is made.

2. **Explain where chloroplasts are found**
   - “Leaves are the most common kitchen, but green stems and green fruits also have kitchens.”
   - Use a real plant (e.g., a tomato plant) to point out green stems and green fruit.

3. **Talk about light**
   - “The kitchen needs light to cook. That’s why photosynthesis only happens when the sun is out.”
   - Mention that plants can store the food they make in the kitchen for later use.

4. **Mention exceptions**
   - Briefly note that some plants (cacti, succulents) can photosynthesize at night (CAM) – a fun fact to spark curiosity.

5. **Interactive activity**
   - Have students measure the amount of sugar in a leaf vs. a stem (using a simple test kit) to see that both can produce food.
