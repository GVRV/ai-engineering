The water cycle is the continuous movement of water between the Earth and the atmosphere. Heat from the Sun causes water from oceans, rivers, lakes, and other sources to change into water vapour. This process is called evaporation. Plants also release water vapour through a process called transpiration. As the water vapour rises and cools, it changes into tiny water droplets and forms clouds. This is called condensation. When the droplets become heavy, they fall to the Earth as rain, snow, or hail. This is called precipitation. Some water flows into rivers and lakes, while some soaks into the ground. The water cycle keeps repeating and provides fresh water for plants, animals, and people.
