A verb is a word that tells us about an action, an event, or a state of being. Verbs are an important part of sentences because they tell us what someone or something does. For example, in the sentence “Riya runs every morning,” the word “runs” is a verb. Other examples of action verbs are jump, write, sing, eat, and swim. Some verbs describe a state rather than an action, such as is, am, are, seem, and become. Verbs can also change their form to show when an action happens. For example, “walk” is used for the present, “walked” for the past, and “will walk” for the future. Using the correct verb helps us clearly express our ideas.
