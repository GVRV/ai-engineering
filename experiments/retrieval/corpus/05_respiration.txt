Human respiration is the process by which our body gets energy from the food we eat. We breathe in air through our nose or mouth. The air travels down the windpipe and reaches the lungs. Inside the lungs, oxygen from the air passes into the blood. The blood carries oxygen to all parts of the body. Our cells use oxygen to break down food and release energy. During this process, carbon dioxide is produced as a waste gas. The blood carries carbon dioxide back to the lungs, where it is removed from the body when we breathe out. The main organs involved in respiration are the nose, windpipe, lungs, and diaphragm.
