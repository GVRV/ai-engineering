Human digestion is the process by which our body breaks down food into smaller substances that it can use for energy, growth, and repair. Digestion begins in the mouth, where our teeth chew food and saliva starts breaking it down. The food then travels through the food pipe to the stomach. In the stomach, digestive juices break the food into smaller parts. The partly digested food moves into the small intestine, where most nutrients are absorbed into the blood. The remaining waste passes into the large intestine, where water is absorbed. Finally, the waste is removed from the body through the rectum and anus. The main organs involved in digestion are the mouth, food pipe, stomach, small intestine, and large intestine.
