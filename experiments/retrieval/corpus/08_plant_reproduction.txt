Plant reproduction is the process by which plants produce new plants of their own kind. Many plants reproduce through flowers. The flower contains special parts called stamens and pistils. The stamen produces pollen, while the pistil contains the ovary and ovules. Pollination happens when pollen moves from the stamen to the pistil, often with the help of insects, birds, wind, or water. After pollination, fertilisation takes place and a seed begins to develop inside the ovary. The ovary may grow into a fruit that protects the seeds. When the seeds are spread and get suitable conditions such as water, air, and warmth, they can grow into new plants. Some plants can also reproduce without seeds through stems, roots, or leaves.
