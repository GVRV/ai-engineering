Mahatma Gandhi was an important leader of India’s freedom movement. He was born on 2 October 1869 in Porbandar, Gujarat. Gandhi believed in truth, peace, and non-violence. He encouraged people to fight against British rule without using violence. He led several important movements, including the Non-Cooperation Movement, the Salt March, and the Quit India Movement. Gandhi also worked to improve the lives of poor people and encouraged Indians to use goods made in their own country. His simple lifestyle and strong beliefs inspired millions of people in India and around the world. Because of his commitment to truth and non-violence, he is remembered as the “Father of the Nation” in India. He died on 30 January 1948.
